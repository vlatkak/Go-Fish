Go Fish

The goal of the game is to collect as many sets of cards as possible. A set of cards consists of 4 cards of the same rank (the symbol in the corners of a card). Once you collect a set it will be removed from your hand.

You ask your opponent for a certain rank, and if he has cards of that rank in his hand, he must give them all to you. If he doesn’t, you draw from the deck. If you receive your desired rank, be it from the opponent or from the deck, you get to go again. Otherwise, it’s the opponents turn.

You play until both of you have run out of cards.
